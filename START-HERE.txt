Charmcaster Avatar v2

Extract this archive completely into a folder, then open index.html in Chrome or Edge.
Allow the camera. All artwork and tracking models are embedded in index.html.
Full-body avatar is enabled by default. Step back to let the camera see your arms and legs.
Use Expression pose to select one of 80 variations. Live speech and blinks remain active.
This is a 2D puppet; extreme turns and crossed limbs may distort it.
