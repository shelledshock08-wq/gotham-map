YOUR CAMERA, TRANSFORMED

Meet your alter ego.

  1. Enable your camera and allow browser access.
  2. Keep your face in frame, with light in front of you.
  3. Try a smile, a blink, an open mouth or a head tilt.
  4. Tap the shutter for a photo. Switch to Video for a clip up to 60 seconds.

Tracking and recording happen on this device. The app never uploads your camera feed. The first camera start loads the bundled tracking models and can take a few seconds.

Front-facing and moderate head turns work best. This is a rigged cartoon head, so a full profile, hands over the face, or very dim light may interrupt tracking.

Use a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari browser. A hosted copy must use HTTPS; localhost is supported for development. Some mobile browsers require the hosted version instead of a downloaded HTML file.
